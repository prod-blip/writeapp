# ClearWrite Google Play Store Listing

## App name

ClearWrite

## Short description

A focused writing editor that checks clarity and helps you improve with AI.

## Full description

ClearWrite is a writing editor aided by AI. Write or paste your draft, understand what could be clearer, and improve only the parts that need attention.

First, ClearWrite checks your writing for:

- Long and difficult sentences
- Long paragraphs
- Repeated words
- Wordy phrases
- Readability and clarity
- Other common writing problems

Tap any issue to move directly to the relevant text. You can edit it yourself or, with ClearWrite Pro, ask AI to rewrite the selected passage while preserving your meaning.

You can also select any part of your draft and use AI to make it clearer, shorter or better suited to your preferred tone. Review every suggestion before deciding whether to apply it.

ClearWrite includes:

- A clean, distraction-free writing editor
- Automatic local writing analysis
- Clarity scoring and direct issue highlighting
- TXT and DOCX imports
- Document titles and on-device saving
- Light and dark themes
- Optional AI rewriting with ClearWrite Pro
- Multiple saved documents and higher word limits with Pro

Your standard writing analysis runs locally on your device. Text is sent to the AI service only when you choose to request an AI suggestion.

Check your writing. Understand what needs improvement. Use AI only when you want help rewriting it.
